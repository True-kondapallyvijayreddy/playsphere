## Fix pass 7 — 2026-10-03 (code review + every still-open FAIL)

**Trigger:** a full code review of the branch after it was committed (`fb46bd2`), cross-checked against this report's Pass 6 list of still-open bugs. Fixed in `b2ca749`, which is **deployed**: Firestore rules and indexes, the five affected Cloud Functions, and hosting.

**New bugs found by the review, not by any earlier pass (confirmed against the live database before fixing):**
- **Five queries failed with "The query requires an index".** They were waitlist promotion after a withdrawal, squad-reserve promotion, and the three sponsorship lists ("My listings", "My pledges", the pledge inbox). The first one is the **index-leak half of TC-ADM-064** that Pass 6 left unconfirmed: an organizer withdrawing a confirmed entry saw the raw Firestore index error, and the first reserve was never promoted. - **Teams promoted off a waitlist were never told.** The notice went to the team's id, not a person. It now goes to whoever entered the team.
- **A scorer could seat any entrant in an empty next-round slot,** and the server-side advancement then refused to correct it. The advancement write must now name the finished match it comes from, and the rules check that this match feeds the slot and produced the entrant.
- **Two co-owners stepping down at the same moment could leave a club with no owner,** and a club could be handed to a child's profile. Step-down is now a transaction over every owner's row. The rules refuse making a known minor an owner.
- **A team's roster changing after entry was never propagated or re-checked.** `onTeamRosterChanged` now updates the team's live entries, its draw entrant and its unplayed fixtures' squads. Anyone newly added who fails the event's age/gender limits is flagged on the entries list, and the person who entered the team is told.
